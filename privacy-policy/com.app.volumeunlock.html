Privacy Policy for Volume Unlock

Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Volume Unlock application developed by Javanware.

Core Principles

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app is designed to function locally on your device to provide utility features.

Device Administrator Permission

Volume Unlock requires Device Administrator permission to function. This is a technical requirement for any app that mimics a power button to lock or wake the screen. We do not use this permission to access any personal data.

Permissions

  • BIND_DEVICE_ADMIN: To lock/wake the screen.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: To start the service automatically after your device restarts (optional).

Data Safety

Standard anonymous data may be collected by Google Play services for crash reporting and installation metrics. Javanware does not have access to personally identifiable information from these services.
