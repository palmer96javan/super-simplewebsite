Privacy Policy for Zoom Player Plus

1. Core Functionality

Zoom Player Plus is a high-performance **HD Video Player and Offline Video Editor Suite**. To provide its core features—including extravagant 500% spatial pinch-to-zoom playback, recursive video file management, and offline media editing (watermarking, cutting, joining, compressing, converting)—the app requires access to media files and storage on your Android device.

2. Storage & File Access

To enable **recursive folder organization**, bulk file renaming, and comprehensive media file operations, the app requests the 'All Files Access' (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) permission on supported Android versions. This level of access is strictly required to manage large media collections across internal and removable storage, as standard scoped media permissions do not support full-suite file management and offline video editing workflows.

We do not collect, transmit, or share any file data, personal content, or file metadata from your device. All media operations and video editing tasks are performed 100% locally on your device.

3. Media Permissions

The app uses **Photos and Videos** permissions strictly for:

  • Playback of local high-definition video files.
  • Generating visual thumbnails for your media library.
  • Performing offline video editing operations (cutting, joining, watermarking, speed adjustments, and conversions).
  • Custom watermark and sticker layer placements.

4. Advertising & Analytics

This Free Edition utilizes third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs)—including Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics—which may collect anonymized device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) to serve personalized advertisements, measure ad performance, and provide crash diagnostics. Users can manage or reset their Advertising ID at any time via Android Device Settings.
