Privacy Policy for Zoom Video Plus

Last Updated: August 18, 2026

1. Core Functionality

Zoom Video Plus is a professional **Video File Manager** and Media Suite. To provide its core features, the app requires access to media files and storage on your device.

2. All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)

Zoom Video Plus includes a specialized File Management module that allows users to organize, rename, and move video files across all device directories. To provide these features, the app requests the **'All Files Access'** permission.

  • Purpose: This permission is used exclusively to facilitate file management tasks initiated by the user, such as bulk renaming, moving files between directories, and recursive scanning of video content.
  • Data Collection: We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any data accessed through this permission. All file operations are performed locally on the user\'s device.
  • User Control: Users can grant or revoke this permission at any time through the Android System Settings. If revoked, the app will continue to function as a basic video player, but advanced file management features will be disabled.

Your privacy is our priority. No file data or metadata is ever shared with external servers or third parties.

3. Media Permissions

The app uses **Photos and Videos** permissions strictly for:

  • Playback of local video files.
  • Creating 'Photo to Video' projects.
  • Generating visual thumbnails for your media library.
  • Performing editing operations like cutting and converting.

4. Advertising & Analytics

This free version uses third-party SDKs (AdMob and Firebase) which may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) to serve personalized ads and provide app performance diagnostics.
