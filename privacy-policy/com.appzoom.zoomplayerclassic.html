Privacy Policy for Zoom Video Player

Introduction

At Javanware, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Zoom Video Player application handles your data.

Data Collection

Zoom Video Player does not collect or share any personal data from users directly. We do not require you to create an account, nor do we ask for sensitive personal files like photos, contacts, or your microphone/camera for anything other than local playback.

Permissions

  • Storage Access: Required to scan and play video files stored on your device.
  • Internet: Used only for standard anonymous crash reporting and basic analytics through Google Firebase.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase to collect anonymous data solely for the purpose of identifying app crashes and improving performance. This data is not linked to your identity.
