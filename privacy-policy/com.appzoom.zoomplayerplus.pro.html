Privacy Policy for Zoom Video Plus (Pro)

Last Updated: August 18, 2026

1. Premium Privacy Commitment

Zoom Video Plus (Pro) is designed for professionals who demand maximum privacy. **This version is 100% Ad-Free and does not contain advertising tracking SDKs.**

2. Professional All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)

As an elite **Video File Manager**, the app requires the **'All Files Access'** permission to perform recursive folder organization, bulk file moves, and renaming across all storage volumes. This level of access is necessary to manage professional media libraries where standard Scoped Storage APIs would cause significant performance limitations.

  • Purpose: This permission is used exclusively to facilitate file management tasks initiated by the user, such as bulk renaming, moving files between directories, and recursive scanning of video content.
  • Data Collection: We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any data accessed through this permission. All file operations are performed locally on the user\'s device.
  • User Control: Users can grant or revoke this permission at any time through the Android System Settings. If revoked, the app will continue to function as a basic video player, but advanced file management features will be disabled.

Your files remain strictly local. We never collect, transmit, or share any of your personal content or file metadata.

3. Media Usage

The app accesses **Photos and Videos** on your device solely to enable its core features: advanced 4x zoom playback, professional editing, and media library management.
