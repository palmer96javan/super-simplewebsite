Privacy Policy for Sentence Dictionary

Our Commitment

Sentence Dictionary is designed as a privacy-first educational tool. We believe in providing value without compromising your personal data.

No Personal Data

The application does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All searches and word lookups happen locally on your device using an offline database.

Internet Usage

While the core dictionary functions offline, the app may use a limited internet connection for:

  • Displaying advertisements to support development.
  • Anonymous performance monitoring and crash reports.

Ad Partners

We use Google AdMob to serve ads. AdMob may use device identifiers to personalize ads. You can manage your ad preferences in your Google Account settings.
